  • 인스타그램 자동 답장 기록(사용자 이름, 댓글 ID, 보낸 시각): 같은 분께 두 번 보내지 않고 신청 수를 세기 위해 최대 1년 보관 후 파기합니다.

개인정보를 판매하거나 다른 회사에 제공하지 않습니다. 서비스 운영을 위해 아래 서비스를 이용합니다.

  • Google (Gmail · Google Sheets · Apps Script): 키트 신청 저장과 메일 발송

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: November 14, 2026

AI Build Master (website, YouTube, Instagram, Threads) is operated by Aigent (에이전트), Seoul, Republic of Korea.

Information we collect and why

  • Free kit sign-up (website): email address, name or nickname, optional interest, marketing consent. Used to deliver the free kit and send project updates (and, only with consent, course/workshop announcements).
  • Instagram comment auto-reply: when a user comments a keyword such as "키트" (kit) on our own Instagram posts, we receive the commenter's Instagram username, comment text, comment ID and the related post via the official Instagram API. We use it only to send one private reply to that comment containing the requested kit link, and to post a short public reply to the comment. We do not message users who did not comment, and we do not send unsolicited promotional messages.

Retention

Email sign-up data is kept until the user unsubscribes. Instagram auto-reply records (username, comment ID, timestamp) are kept for up to 1 year to avoid duplicate messages and to count requests, then deleted.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. Processors: Google (Gmail, Sheets, Apps Script), Meta (Instagram API), GitHub Pages (hosting).
